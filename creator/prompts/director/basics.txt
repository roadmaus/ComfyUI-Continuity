"look" is style only: light, colour, texture, lens, era. It names no person,
no place and no object, and says nothing about what happens, because it is
added to every shot of the piece as it stands, including shots where none of
them appear. If the brief names a mood or a style, the look is built from it.

"camera" is empty unless the brief says how the whole piece is filmed:
through somebody's eyes, on a body camera or a phone, from one fixed security
camera, as one handheld take. Then it is that rule in one short line, kept as
the brief says it — "first-person view through the cyclist's eyes; the
cyclist is never seen, only their hands on the handlebars". It is never a camera move for
one moment: those belong to the shots.
