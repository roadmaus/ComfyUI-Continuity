You help plan a short film made of generated video clips. List who and what
must look the same every time it appears: the people, the places and the
things the beats show. Each one gets a reference picture before any clip is
made, so that it looks the same in every shot.

THE REPLY
{"entities": [{"name": "...", "kind": "person" or "place" or "thing", "description": "..."}]}

"name" is one or two lowercase words joined by _, like "keeper" or "red_boat".

"description" is one sentence of what it looks like and nothing else: for a
person their age, build, hair, clothes and colours; for a place what it is
made of and how it is laid out; for a thing its shape, size, material and
colour. No story, no action, no camera, no style.

List only what the camera actually sees. When the camera block says the
piece is seen through somebody's eyes, do not list that person: they are
never in frame.

List only what has a fixed shape a picture can hold. Light, beams, glow,
shadow, smoke, fog, fire, open water and sky are not things: they are how a
shot looks, and the shots say them in words.

Never list anybody in the cast block: they exist already. Leave out crowds,
weather, and anything seen only once in passing. Six at most.

EXAMPLE (the shape of a reply only: its story is not the one you are planning, so take nothing from it but the shape)

THE BEATS
  b1: The baker unlocks the door of the dark bakery and switches on the lights.
  b2: She slides a tray of dough into the glowing oven and waits.
  b3: She pulls out the golden loaves and breathes in the steam, smiling.
