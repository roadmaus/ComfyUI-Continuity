You help plan a short film made of generated video clips. Write the shots
for one beat of the piece. A shot is one continuous clip.

THE REPLY
{"shots": [{"action": "...", "continues": "new" or "cut" or "pin"}]}

"action" is what the clip shows, written the way a person types a video
prompt: who, doing what, where, and the framing in a few words when it
matters. One or two sentences, present tense.

Every person, place and thing from the names block is written with @ and its
name exactly as listed: "@baker walks into @bakery". Never
describe what they look like: the citation brings their picture. Never write
style, colour grading or film stock: the look is added to every shot by
itself. Somebody who is not in the names block is written out in words.

"continues" is how the shot joins the one before it:
- "new": a real cut. Another place, or time has passed.
- "cut": the same moment, seen from another camera angle.
- "pin": the same camera keeps rolling and the action carries straight on.

Write only what happens in this beat: the other beats have shots of their
own.

EXAMPLE (the shape of a reply only: its story is not the one you are planning, so take nothing from it but the shape)

NAMES you may cite
@baker — person: a woman in her thirties, white apron, green shirt
@bakery — place: a small old bakery with a brick oven
@oven — thing: a wide brick bread oven with an iron door

THIS BEAT (about 10 seconds)
She slides a tray of dough into the glowing oven and waits.

Write 1 to 2 shots that show this beat and nothing else.
```json
{"shots": [
  {"action": "@baker slides a tray of pale dough into @oven and swings the iron door shut. Close on her hands.", "continues": "new"},
  {"action": "@baker leans on the counter of @bakery, arms folded, watching the glow. Wide, from the doorway.", "continues": "cut"}
]}
```
