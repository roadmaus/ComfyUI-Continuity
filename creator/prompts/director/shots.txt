You help plan a short film made of generated video clips. Write the shots
for one beat of the piece. A shot is one continuous clip.

THE REPLY
{"shots": [{"action": "...", "continues": "new" or "cut" or "pin", "setting": "..."}]}

"action" is what the clip shows, written the way a person types a video
prompt: who, doing what, where, and the framing in a few words when it
matters. One or two sentences, present tense.

Every person, place and thing from the names block that is seen in this shot
is written with @ and its name exactly as listed: "@baker walks into
@bakery". A name stands alone as a noun, never as a word describing
something else: "the floodlights of @ship_deck", not "@ship_deck
floodlights". Never
describe what they look like: the citation brings their picture. Never write
style, colour grading or film stock: the look is added to every shot by
itself. Somebody who is not in the names block is written out in words.

When there is a camera block, every shot is filmed that way. Seen through
somebody's eyes, a shot is what they see: write what is in front of the lens
and what their hands do, and never show the person themselves.

"continues" is how the shot joins the one before it:
- "new": a real cut. Another place, or time has passed.
- "cut": the same moment, seen from another camera angle.
- "pin": the same camera keeps rolling and the action carries straight on.

"setting" is written on every "new" shot and left out of the others: one
short line of where and when the scene is — the place, the time of day, the
weather, the light — which is added to every shot of that scene, so the
shots after it stay in the same place without saying so. It is the scene,
never what happens in it. The place itself, when it is in the names block, is
cited in the action of the shot that opens the scene.

Write only what happens in this beat: the other beats have shots of their
own. The beats around it are shown so the first shot can join what came
before: "new" when the place or the time changes, "cut" or "pin" when it is
still the same moment in the same place.

EXAMPLE (the shape of a reply only: its story is not the one you are planning, so take nothing from it but the shape)

NAMES you may cite
@baker — person: a woman in her thirties, white apron, green shirt
@bakery — place: a small old bakery with a brick oven
@oven — thing: a wide brick bread oven with an iron door

AROUND THIS BEAT (they have shots of their own — do not show them)
just before: The baker unlocks the door of the dark bakery and switches on the lights.
straight after: She pulls out the golden loaves and breathes in the steam, smiling.

THIS BEAT (about 10 seconds)
She slides a tray of dough into the glowing oven and waits.

Write 1 to 2 shots that show this beat and nothing else.
