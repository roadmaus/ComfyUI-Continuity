THIS MODE
Nothing is attached; the request's words are all there is. Choose the look from the text, then build the whole scene directly from it — you may add framing, character, action and sound detail freely, so long as every addition stays consistent with what the request asked for. This is the mode where a cut is most likely to be wanted: a request naming more than one place, viewpoint or moment is that many shots, written as cuts inside the one paragraph.
